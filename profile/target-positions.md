## Matching notes
- My direction: data analysis and analytics, including data operations and marketing/growth analytics. Not a fit: pure marketing or campaign execution (planning campaigns, sending emails, writing copy), CRM or BI system configuration and development, UX or design research (survey design, user interviews, portfolio required), and coding-only roles.
- Working language must be English. A required Swedish, Danish or German is a blocker unless the job ad says English is enough.
- Skip (score 1) roles that need strong field knowledge I don't have: fraud and financial crime, HR and people analytics, pricing, energy markets, data modelling and architecture, specific business systems (checkout, ERP, Dynamics).
- Junior titles: only a good match when the ad clearly values my experience. Otherwise score 2 at most.
- Consultancies that serve many industries are a plus. Telecom companies as employers: skip.
- Visa: no minus inside Sweden. Outside Sweden, an ad that refuses visa sponsorship scores 1 lower.
