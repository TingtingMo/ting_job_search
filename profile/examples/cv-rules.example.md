# My CV and writing rules

## CV

- Headline: "Data Analyst" or the job title from the ad.
- Keep the CV to one page; pick the 4–5 stories that fit the job best.
- Always give a result with its context, e.g. "3 percentage points" not "3%" for conversion.
- Never call myself "expert" or "passionate".

## Cover letter

- Three parts: why I like this role, why I'm a good fit, what I can bring.
- Open with "Hi {Company} team," and close with "Best," and my name.
- Match the tone of my sample below, but don't reuse its sentences.

## Voice

Plain, friendly, first person, concrete. Short sentences. Everyday words.

> I like questions that start simple and end with a number people trust. Most of my favourite work started with someone asking "why did this drop?" and ended with a small change that the whole team could see in the dashboard the next week.
